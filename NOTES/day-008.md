# 🧠 What I Learned

## Reentrancy
- The attacker's `receive()` runs when the bank sends ETH with `call`, and calls `withdraw()` again before the balance was reduced. The `require` passes again because the ledger still shows the old balance.
- Defence: check → update state → send. The attacker's second call then fails on `require("Not enough amount")`.
- `transfer` forwards only 2300 gas, so a contract cannot re-enter; `call` forwards all available gas.
- The attack is a loop bounded by the bank's balance and gas, not infinite. `receive()` needs an `if` as a stop condition, not a `require` (a revert would undo the whole attack).
- Bug I made: the stop condition checked `address(this).balance` (the attacker's own balance) instead of `address(bank).balance`.

## Solidity 0.8 checked arithmetic
- Since 0.8, `+`/`-` revert on overflow/underflow. With the `-=` after the `call`, the attack reverted at the unwinding `0 - 1`, by accident. With `unchecked { ... }` the attack drained the bank. Checked arithmetic is not a reentrancy defence.
- Observed: bank 6 ETH → 0; attacker 3 → 6 ETH; Alice's ledger still said 3 ETH (the ledger lied, the bank was insolvent) and her `withdraw` then reverted.
- The revert message seen at the top (`Transfer failed`) hid the real reason (`Not enough amount` in the inner call).

## Test vocabulary (cheat sheet)
- `getSigners()` = test wallets (`signer.address`, no await); `getContractFactory("Name", signer?)` = blueprint, signer = deployer; `contract.connect(signer)` = calls from that signer.
- `ethers.provider.getBalance(address)` = real ETH; the contract's own `getBalance(user)` = its ledger. They diverged during the attack.
- Contract address: `await contract.getAddress()`; wallet address: `signer.address`.
- `parseEther("1")` → wei (BigInt); `formatEther(x)` → string like `"3.0"`.
- Three forms: read `expect(await c.fn()).to.equal(v)`; must succeed `await c.fn()`; must fail `await expect(c.fn()).to.be.revertedWith("msg")` (no await inside).

## Tooling
- In the Bank project, `revertedWith` was "Invalid Chai property" whenever Hardhat printed "Nothing to compile". Adding `require("@nomicfoundation/hardhat-chai-matchers");` at the top of each test file fixed it reliably. Root cause not fully identified.

# ⚠️ Mistakes To Remember

- Recall: contract "participates in the game" (it holds the pot; players participate); `await` inside `expect` (again); `transfer` explained by the amount instead of the 2300 gas limit; gas limit called a price.
- No prediction written before running tests, twice.
- Said "everything works / tout est réglé" while tests were red (no-arg run); always paste the real output.
- Exercise answers from the vocabulary drill: line 2 correct; line 1 had an extra `)`, missing `()` after `getAddress`, missing `await` on the balance, `await` on the synchronous `formatEther`, and `"3"` instead of `"3.0"`; line 3 used `"5 ether"` (Solidity syntax) instead of `ethers.parseEther("5")`. The `await`/`expect` forms were right.
- Changed two things at once (`unchecked` and the attack amount), which blurs the proof.
