# 🧠 What I Learned

## Gas
- Gas = unit of computation. Gas price = what I pay **per unit**. Gas limit = **maximum** units I accept to spend.
- Total cost = gas used × gas price.
- Low price + congested mempool → transaction waits. Limit too low → out of gas, state reverted, ETH value returns, **gas already used is lost**.
- Work done is always paid, even if the transaction fails.

## Lottery contract
- `msg.sender` = address of the caller. In the constructor, it is the deployer.
- `require(cond, msg)` continues only if `cond` is **true**; otherwise reverts. `> 0.1 ether` rejects exactly 0.1 — changed to `>= 0.1 ether`.
- `payable` = the function may receive ETH (`msg.value`). It has nothing to do with paying gas.
- `view` function = read-only, free when called from outside (`call`).
- `modifier restricted` = reusable check; `_;` marks where the function body runs.

# ⚠️ Mistakes To Remember

- Said gas limit is the "minimum" → it is the **maximum**.
- Said gas limit should be above gas price → they are different units (gas units vs gwei), not comparable.
- Said the 20,000 unused units "come back" → they were never executed, nothing to refund.
- Mixed up `payable` with paying gas.
- Reversed the meaning of `require`.
- Said a reverted call from account B costs no gas → **B still pays the gas used until the revert**.
